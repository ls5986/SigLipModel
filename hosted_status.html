The review workflow

Step 1Confirm the photos

Verify these are the correct property and acquisition-era photos. Mark later renovations, floor plans, amenities, and unrelated images so they cannot teach condition.

Step 2Rate the property

Score whether the property is worth investigating. This is a property label; it does not automatically label every photo.

Step 3Teach individual evidence

Select standout photos and correct photo context, room, and opportunity when needed. Approved corrections become candidate training labels.

MLS metadata

The full sanitized listing snapshot is retained with the confirmed listing. The current metadata candidate uses 12 pre-decision feature groups, not every field indiscriminately.

Identifiers, private/contact fields, URLs, and later-sale outcome fields are excluded from model input. Additional safe MLS fields can be added under a versioned feature policy after leakage review.

When a property becomes trainable

  • The property/listing identity and acquisition era are confirmed.
  • The property has a decisive rating (1–2 or 4–5); “Maybe” and insufficient evidence stay unknown.
  • Wrong-era, amenity, floor-plan, and unrelated photos are excluded.
  • The approved property label is joined to its immutable pre-decision metadata snapshot.
  • A new candidate is trained and evaluated by physical-property group before promotion.
